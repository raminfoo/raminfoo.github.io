1. Information We Collect

Our applications are designed to provide a useful and enjoyable user experience. Depending on an application’s features and the services integrated into it, information that may be processed includes:

  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, application version and general device information.
  • Usage information: App interactions, gameplay progress, session information and feature usage.
  • Diagnostic information: Crash reports, performance information and error logs.
  • Advertising information: Advertising identifiers and related information used to display and measure advertisements, where applicable.
  • Information you provide: Information you voluntarily submit through support or feedback features, if available.

Not every application processes every category listed above. The information processed depends on the features, permissions and third-party services used by that particular application.

2. Offline Functionality and Local Storage

Many BUSYLIFE applications and games are designed to work offline. Depending on the application, information such as game progress, scores, preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device.

We do not require users to create an account in applications that do not provide account-based features. Uninstalling an application may remove information stored locally by that application. Data stored or backed up by your device or a third-party service may be subject to that service’s own practices.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some BUSYLIFE applications may display advertisements to support their development and maintenance. Where integrated, we may use Google AdMob or other advertising providers.

Depending on the provider, device settings, consent choices and applicable law, advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers;
  • Device and application information;
  • Advertising views, clicks and interaction information;
  • IP address and approximate location that may be derived from it; and
  • Information used for advertising measurement, security and fraud prevention.

Advertising providers may use information to provide, personalize, limit or measure advertisements, subject to the applicable settings, consent requirements and provider policies.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · Google AdMob.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Some applications may integrate analytics and diagnostic services such as Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or other performance-monitoring services. If used, these services may process application usage, technical information and crash reports to help us understand performance, identify problems and improve the user experience.

The services used vary by application. For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information. Where necessary to operate an application, information may be processed or shared with service providers for purposes such as:

  • Displaying and measuring advertisements;
  • Analyzing application performance;
  • Identifying and fixing technical issues;
  • Providing and maintaining application functionality; and
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party providers handle information under their own privacy policies, terms and applicable legal requirements.

6. Children’s Privacy

The intended audience may differ between BUSYLIFE applications and is described in the relevant Google Play listing and app content. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children except as permitted by applicable law and with any required consent or authorization.

Applications directed to children, or that include children in their target audience, must use appropriate child-directed settings, consent mechanisms and third-party SDK configurations as required by applicable law and Google Play policies.

Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern about a child’s use of an application may contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through our applications. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally on your device generally remains there until it is deleted by you or the application is uninstalled. Information processed by third-party services may be retained in accordance with those providers’ retention policies.

If an application provides an account or online service, additional data access or deletion options may apply. You may contact us for assistance with applicable privacy requests. Where information is held by a third-party provider, its own deletion and retention processes may also apply.

9. Permissions

Our applications request permissions that are intended to support their functionality. The permissions requested differ between applications. You can review and manage app permissions through your Android device settings.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our applications may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or policies of external websites and services. Please review the relevant third party’s privacy policy before using its services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our applications, third-party services, legal requirements or business practices. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
